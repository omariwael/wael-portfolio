About

I work across two worlds most people keep separate: the craft of visual design, and the evidence behind how people learn. That means a logotype one week and an interactive training module the next — and the conviction that both should be held to the same standard.

I started with a pen. Arabic calligraphy taught me proportion, rhythm, and patience long before I opened a design program, and it still decides how I judge a curve or a counter. It's why my identities carry a hand, and why type is never an afterthought in my work.

Most recently I spent eleven years at the Institute of Public Administration in Riyadh, where I founded the in-house design team and led it for four years while still designing daily. Alongside that, I'm completing a PhD in Creative Multimedia, researching how augmented reality changes the way people learn together.
